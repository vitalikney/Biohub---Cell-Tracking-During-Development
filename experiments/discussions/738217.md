<p>The trick to transforming Focus3D annotation to Kaggle-like annotation<br>
1) Just pretrain with Focus3D annotation<br>
2) find matches (hit) of Focus3D and Kaggle zyx to compute diff dzyx<br>
3) have two heads: one to predict Focus3D zyx, another head dzyx, then Kagge zyx = Focus3D +dzyx  </p>
<p>hint: might as well let diff head predict values in um (instead of quantized 64x64x64 coord) </p>
<p><img src="https://www.googleapis.com/download/storage/v1/b/kaggle-forum-message-attachments/o/inbox%2F113660%2Fe52d3a2127e86cfcfcf93c3c78f9cf1d%2FSelection_4791.png?generation=1788637476144738&amp;alt=media" alt=""></p>

3) if you analyse post-processing, you will find that they use heuristics to join cells if the broken gap interval is   small.
