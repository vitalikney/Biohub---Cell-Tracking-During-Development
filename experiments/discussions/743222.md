<p>We tried for two weeks to beat the public dual-seed notebook (UNet + node transformer + ILP) and never did. Every change we tested scored at or below the unmodified notebook. Posting the numbers so nobody else spends the same two weeks.</p>
<h2>Local scores did not predict the leaderboard</h2>

<p>Both threshold directions lose, so 0.965 is already at the optimum. Extra TTA views buy nothing. Every change that trusts the learned association more (relink off, lower candidate threshold) failed, while the geometric repair always helped — which fits test being a different embryo.</p>
<h2>A trap inside the notebook worth knowing about</h2>
<p>The notebook scores 8 train videos at the end, sweeps 7 post-processing candidates, and rewrites <code>submission.csv</code> with the winner. Two problems:</p>
<ol>
<li>Those 8 videos are the ones the secondary model memorised.</li>
<li>The list excludes train stems that also appear in <code>TEST_DIR</code>. On your commit run that drops the 4 example videos. On the hidden rerun there is no overlap, so <strong>a different 8 videos are scored and a different config can win</strong>. The setup that produced your leaderboard score may not be the one printed in your log.</li>
</ol>
<p>We only found this after pinning the selected config by hand and losing 0.002.</p>
<h2>Training our own model</h2>
<p>We started from the public secondary checkpoint and adapted it for 6 hours at lr 1e-5 on 600 synthetic sequences (CC0 set by <a href="/Jos" target="_blank">@Jos</a>é Freitas) mixed with 40 real videos. Swapped in: <strong>0.939</strong>, about 0.006 worse than the public weights.</p>
<h2>If you plan to train on Kaggle GPUs</h2>
<p>On 2× T4 with the support-pack trainer, 64³ windows: batch 16 runs out of memory, and before it died it ran at <strong>12–14 seconds per step</strong>, about 35–40 minutes per epoch. An 8-hour session buys 12–25 epochs. The public primary had 402. That gap is not closable on Kaggle hardware.</p>
<h2>What we would do differently</h2>
<p>Stop tuning inference and measure properly: train on one embryo, evaluate on the other. It is the only split in this dataset that is not memorised.</p>
